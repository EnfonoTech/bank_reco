### Features

* action log of every reconciliation, with one-click Undo and batch Undo (History page, Ctrl/Cmd+Z)
* bank rules can be Suggest Only: matching transactions are flagged and a user applies the rule with one click; optional hourly evaluation
* statement closing balance is saved per bank account and date
* banking reports (Bank Reconciliation Statement, Bank Clearance Summary, Cheques and Deposits Incorrectly cleared) inside Bank Reco
* internal transfers: mirror transaction detection across bank accounts and one-step transfer + reconcile
* statement importer understands CR/DR amount markers
* search box on the transaction list; rule-suggested rows pre-fill Bank Coding
