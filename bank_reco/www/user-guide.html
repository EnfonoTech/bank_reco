1. Before you start

Bank Reco works on Bank Transactions (the lines of your bank statement) and links them to ERPNext vouchers: Payment Entry, Journal Entry, Sales Invoice, Purchase Invoice.

  1. Create the Bank and a Bank Account (Accounts > Bank Account).
  2. Tick Is Company Account and select the Account (the ledger account of that bank). Only Bank Accounts that are company accounts and have a ledger account are listed in Bank Reco. If the screen says No company bank accounts found, this is why.
  3. For a credit card, tick Credit Card on the Bank Account so imported deposits and withdrawals are flipped to the right side.
  4. Import the statement (section 3) or create Bank Transactions by hand. Transactions must be submitted.

3. Import a statement

Open the Import tab. No desk screen is needed.

  1. Select the company and bank account.
  2. Drop the statement file (CSV, XLSX or XLS) on the page, or click Choose file.
  3. Match the columns. Date, description, amount (or deposit and withdrawal) are detected for you, and the layout you confirmed last time for this bank is reused. Fix any column that is wrong; set the date format if the dates look wrong.
  4. Amounts can be one signed column, two columns, or text with a marker such as 1,250.00 CR / DR 99.50. A CR/DR marker decides the column (CR = deposit, DR = withdrawal) whatever the sign says.
  5. Preview import. You see how many rows are new, how many already exist, the deposit and withdrawal totals and the date range. Duplicate rows are greyed out.
  6. Tick Remember this column layout and click Import. The import runs on the server with a progress bar (a few hundred rows take a minute or two) and keeps going if you leave the page. Then Reconcile these transactions opens the Reconcile tab on the imported period.
For a credit card account (Credit Card ticked on the Bank Account) deposits and withdrawals are swapped on import, and the preview tells you so.

4. Reconcile (Match tab)

  1. Pick a bank account and dates. Click a transaction in the left list. Use the search box to find by description, reference, party or amount.
  2. The Match tab lists candidate vouchers ranked by amount, party and reference, with a confidence label. Unpaid Sales / Purchase Invoices (and credit / debit notes) are included.
  3. Tick one or several vouchers, adjust the allocated amount if needed, then Reconcile. For unpaid invoices the system creates the Payment Entry for you.
  4. A green banner appears with an Undo button for the action you just did.
The Update tab edits the reference number, party type and party of the bank transaction. The Details tab shows the linked payments.

5. Create a voucher from a transaction

When nothing matches, open the Create tab, choose the ledger account (Journal Entry) or a party (Payment Entry), fill the cost centre, project and accounting dimensions, and submit. Use Save as draft to finish the voucher in the desk. Tick Save as rule to create a bank rule from this entry.

6. Internal transfers

  1. Select the withdrawal (or deposit) that is a move between two of your own accounts and open the Transfer tab.
  2. Bank Reco looks for the mirror transaction in another bank account: same company and currency, same amount, opposite direction, within the match window (Settings, default 4 days).
  3. Select it and click Create transfer and reconcile. One Internal Transfer Payment Entry is posted and both bank transactions are reconciled.
  4. If the other side has no imported transaction, enter the other Bank/Cash ledger account instead.

7. Bank Coding

For bank charges, interest and similar lines. Choose the ledger account (and party for receivable / payable accounts) on each row, select the rows and submit: one Journal Entry per row. Rows flagged Rule suggested are pre-filled from a matching bank rule. Each row succeeds or fails on its own.

8a. Auto reconcile

Instead of choosing a voucher for each line, Auto reconcile (Reconcile tab) matches unreconciled transactions of the selected dates to existing vouchers by itself.

  1. Click Auto reconcile. The window lists the rules it will apply, taken from Advance Bank Reconciliation Settings > Auto Reconcile.
  2. Preview matches shows what would be matched and why (nothing is changed), then Reconcile now does it. It runs on the server with a progress bar.
  3. Undo all reverses the whole run; each match is also in History.

On the Import tab you can also tick Run auto rules afterwards and Auto reconcile afterwards so a freshly imported statement reconciles itself. Auto rules (bank rules in Auto Execute mode, section 8) post new vouchers for charges and similar lines; Auto reconcile only links vouchers that already exist.

8. Bank rules

A rule says: if the description contains X, post to account Z.

Create a rule in three clicks

  1. On the Reconcile tab select a transaction (for example a bank charge) and click New rule. The description and direction are pre-filled from it. Or open the Rules tab and click New rule.
  2. Check Direction and Description contains; add more conditions if needed (amount, reference ... with "match any" or "match all").
  3. Choose what to create (Journal Entry to an Account, or Payment Entry for a Party), the cost centre / project, and the Mode. Click Test on unreconciled to see how many open transactions it matches, then Create rule.

On the Rules tab you can also Edit, Disable / Enable and Delete a rule and move it up or down: rules are checked from the top and the first match wins. The mode of a rule is:

ModeWhat happens
Auto ExecuteRun auto rules (Rules tab) creates and reconciles the voucher for every matching transaction in the date range. Asks for confirmation first.
Suggest OnlyNothing is posted. Matching transactions are flagged with the rule name. Select one and click Apply (posts it) or Dismiss.
  • Run rules (Reconcile) and Suggest matches (Rules) flag matches without posting anything.
  • Settings > Evaluate bank rules hourly does the flagging automatically; it never posts.
  • Rules are checked by priority (lowest number first); the first match wins.
Test an Auto Execute rule on a few transactions first: it posts real vouchers. Everything it posts can be undone from History.

9. History and Undo

Every match, created voucher, bank-coding row, rule run and transfer is logged. Open History (or press Ctrl/Cmd+Z outside a text box).

  • Undo on a row reverses that action for that transaction. Undo batch reverses a whole bank-coding run or rule run.
  • Vouchers the action created are cancelled. Vouchers it only matched are just unlinked and stay in the books.
  • An undo is refused if something else was linked to the transaction afterwards; use the Matched tab then.
  • Undoing a transfer reverses both sides and cancels the Payment Entry.
  • "Only my actions" is on by default.

10. Statement balance

Type the closing balance printed on the statement into Statement closing balance. It is saved for that bank account and the To date, and shown again next time. The Difference card compares it with the ERP cleared balance and turns green (Balanced) when they agree. Clear the field to delete the saved value.

11. Reports

The Reports tab runs ERPNext's own reports for the selected bank account: Bank Reconciliation Statement (as of the To date), Bank Clearance Summary (From to To) and Cheques and Deposits Incorrectly cleared (as of the To date).

13. Roles

Bank Reco is available to System Manager, Accounts Manager and Accounts User. Users also need permission on the company, the Bank Account and the vouchers involved; company user permissions are respected everywhere.

14. Troubleshooting

SymptomFix
"No company bank accounts found"Open the Bank Account, tick Is Company Account and set the ledger Account.
Transaction list is emptyWiden the dates, set Status to All, and check the Bank Transactions are submitted.
Undo is refusedAnother voucher was linked to that transaction later. Unreconcile from the Matched tab.
Selected amount exceeds the unallocated amountLower the allocation or untick a voucher.
Rule never suggestsThe rule must be enabled, belong to this bank account, and use the exact field names (Description, Deposit, Withdrawal ...). Click Suggest matches on the Rules tab.
Progress dialog never closesCheck that the background worker is running; or switch off background reconciliation in Settings.
